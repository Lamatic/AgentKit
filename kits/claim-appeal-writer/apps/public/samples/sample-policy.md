## SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Medically Necessary / Medical Necessity.** Health care services that a Physician, exercising prudent clinical judgment, would provide to a patient for the purpose of preventing, evaluating, diagnosing or treating an illness, injury, disease or its symptoms, and that are: (a) in accordance with generally accepted standards of medical practice; (b) clinically appropriate, in terms of type, frequency, extent, site and duration, and considered effective for the patient's illness, injury or disease; and (c) not primarily for the convenience of the patient, Physician, or other health care provider, and not more costly than an alternative service or sequence of services at least as likely to produce equivalent therapeutic or diagnostic results. Generally accepted standards of medical practice means standards that are based on credible scientific evidence published in peer-reviewed medical literature generally recognized by the relevant medical community, Physician Specialty Society recommendations, and the views of Physicians practicing in relevant clinical areas.

**1.2 Prior Authorization.** The process by which the Plan determines, before a service is rendered, whether the service is Medically Necessary and a Covered Service.

**1.3 Emergency Services.** Services needed to evaluate or stabilize an Emergency Medical Condition, meaning a condition manifesting itself by acute symptoms of sufficient severity (including severe pain) such that a prudent layperson could reasonably expect the absence of immediate medical attention to result in serious jeopardy to health.

**1.4 Network Provider.** A Physician, hospital or other provider that has a contract with the Plan to provide Covered Services to Members.

**1.5 Experimental or Investigational.** A drug, device, treatment or procedure that (a) is not recognized as safe and effective by the relevant governmental regulatory body for the condition being treated; or (b) is the subject of an ongoing Phase I or Phase II clinical trial; or (c) is not supported by credible scientific evidence published in peer-reviewed literature. A service is NOT Experimental or Investigational solely because it is new or because the Plan has not previously paid for it.

## SECTION 2. ELIGIBILITY AND EFFECTIVE DATE

**2.1** Coverage begins on the first day of the month following the date the Employee's enrollment form is received by the Plan, provided the Employee has satisfied the Employer's waiting period.

**2.2** Coverage for a Dependent child continues to the end of the month in which the child turns 26.

---

## SECTION 3. PRIOR AUTHORIZATION REQUIREMENTS

**3.1 Services Requiring Prior Authorization.** The following require Prior Authorization: inpatient admissions; advanced imaging (MRI, MRA, CT, PET) performed in an outpatient hospital setting; durable medical equipment over $1,000; home health care beyond 30 visits; and non-emergency air ambulance.

**3.2 Provider Responsibility.** When a Member receives services from a Network Provider, it is the Network Provider's responsibility to obtain Prior Authorization. **A Member will not be held financially responsible for a Network Provider's failure to obtain Prior Authorization for an otherwise Covered Service.**

**3.3 Emergency Exception.** Prior Authorization is not required for Emergency Services. Notification within 48 hours of an emergency admission is requested but failure to notify will not by itself result in denial of Medically Necessary services.

**3.4 Retrospective Review.** Where Prior Authorization was not obtained, the Plan will conduct a retrospective review upon request and will approve the claim if the service was Medically Necessary and would have been authorized had a request been made in advance.

## SECTION 4. COVERED SERVICES

**4.1 Diagnostic Imaging.** Covered Services include X-ray, ultrasound, MRI, CT, PET and nuclear imaging when Medically Necessary and ordered by a Physician. Advanced imaging for musculoskeletal conditions is Medically Necessary when conservative treatment (such as physical therapy, anti-inflammatory medication, or activity modification) has been attempted for at least six (6) weeks without adequate improvement, OR when red-flag symptoms are present (including but not limited to progressive neurological deficit, suspected fracture, suspected malignancy, or suspected infection).

**4.2 Physician Services.** Office visits, consultations, and procedures performed by a Physician, including specialist care with or without referral.

**4.3 Outpatient Surgery.** Surgical procedures performed in an ambulatory surgery center or outpatient hospital department.

**4.4 Physical, Occupational and Speech Therapy.** Up to 60 combined visits per Plan Year when Medically Necessary to restore function lost due to illness or injury.

**4.5 Prescription Drugs.** Drugs listed on the Plan Formulary. Non-formulary drugs are covered when the prescribing Physician certifies that formulary alternatives have been tried and failed or are contraindicated.

**4.6 Emergency Services.** Emergency Services are covered at the Network benefit level regardless of whether the provider is a Network Provider.

## SECTION 5. OUT-OF-NETWORK BENEFITS

**5.1 General.** Services from providers that are not Network Providers are covered at the Out-of-Network benefit level (60% of the Allowed Amount after deductible), except as provided in 5.2 and 5.3.

**5.2 Network Adequacy.** If no Network Provider with the appropriate training and experience is available within 30 miles of the Member's home, or within 15 business days for non-urgent care, services from a non-Network Provider will be paid at the Network benefit level. The Member or Physician must request this determination, which the Plan will make within 5 business days.

**5.3 Services at Network Facilities.** When a Member receives services at a Network hospital or Network ambulatory surgery center, services from non-Network providers at that facility (including anesthesiology, radiology, pathology, and assistant surgeons) are covered at the Network benefit level and the Member is responsible only for Network cost-sharing.

**5.4 Continuity of Care.** A Member undergoing an active course of treatment with a provider who leaves the Network may continue to receive services from that provider at the Network benefit level for up to 90 days, or through the postpartum period for a Member in the second or third trimester of pregnancy.

## SECTION 6. EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS

The following are not Covered Services:

**6.1** Services that are not Medically Necessary, as defined in Section 1.1.
**6.2** Cosmetic procedures, except reconstructive surgery following mastectomy, accidental injury, or to correct a congenital anomaly.
**6.3** Experimental or Investigational services as defined in Section 1.5.
**6.4** Custodial care.
**6.5** Services received before the Member's effective date or after termination of coverage.
**6.6** Charges in excess of the Allowed Amount, except as provided in Section 5.3.

## SECTION 7. CLAIMS

**7.1 Filing Deadline.** Claims must be submitted within 12 months of the date of service. Claims submitted after this period will be denied unless the Member demonstrates that it was not reasonably possible to file within that time.

**7.2 Corrected Claims.** A provider or Member may submit a corrected claim within 12 months of the original determination to correct coding or billing errors. A corrected claim is reviewed as a new claim and is not an appeal.

**7.3 Claim Determinations.** The Plan will issue a written determination within 30 days of receiving a post-service claim. Any adverse determination will state the specific reason, reference the specific Plan provision on which the determination is based, describe any additional information needed, and explain the Member's appeal rights.

---

## SECTION 8. APPEALS AND GRIEVANCES

**8.1 Right to Appeal.** A Member may appeal any Adverse Benefit Determination, including a denial based on Medical Necessity, Experimental or Investigational status, or a determination that a service is not a Covered Service.

**8.2 Internal Appeal Deadline.** A request for internal appeal must be received within **180 days** of the date of the adverse determination notice.

**8.3 Full and Fair Review.** The appeal will be reviewed by an individual who was not involved in the initial determination and who is not a subordinate of that individual. Where the determination involved medical judgment, the reviewer will be a health care professional with appropriate training and experience in the relevant field of medicine.

**8.4 Access to the Claim File.** Upon request and free of charge, the Member is entitled to copies of all documents, records, and other information relevant to the claim, including the specific clinical criteria, internal rules, guidelines or protocols relied upon, and the identity of any medical expert consulted.

**8.5 Timeframes.** The Plan will decide a pre-service appeal within 30 days and a post-service appeal within 60 days of receipt. An **expedited appeal** is available, with a decision within 72 hours, where the standard timeframe would seriously jeopardize the Member's life, health, or ability to regain maximum function.

**8.6 External Review.** If the internal appeal upholds a denial based on Medical Necessity, appropriateness, health care setting, level of care, effectiveness, or Experimental or Investigational status, the Member may request an independent external review by an Independent Review Organization within **4 months** of receiving the final internal adverse determination. The external reviewer's decision is binding on the Plan.

**8.7 Authorized Representative.** A Member may designate a Physician, family member, or other person to act on their behalf in the appeal.
